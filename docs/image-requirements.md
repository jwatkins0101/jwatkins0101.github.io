# CoreFour Advisors - Image Requirements & AI Prompts

## Brand Context

### Logo Design
The CoreFour logo features a **diamond/rhombus shape rotated 45 degrees**, composed of **4 triangular segments** arranged in a pinwheel pattern. This represents the "four" in CoreFour and symbolizes the four disciplines (PE, AI, UX, Operations) converging.

### Color Palette
| Color | Hex Code | Usage |
|-------|----------|-------|
| **Oxford Navy** | #0A192F | Primary brand color, text, backgrounds |
| **Warm Gold/Tan** | #C9A962 | Logo accent, premium feel |
| **Signal Blue** | #3B82F6 | Digital accents, CTAs, links |
| **Steel Slate** | #64748B | Secondary text, borders |
| **Platinum** | #F1F5F9 | Light backgrounds |
| **White** | #FFFFFF | Logo segment, text on dark |

### Style Guidelines
- **Aesthetic:** Institutional, minimal, data-driven, "Deep Blue Diligence"
- **Logo motif:** 4-part diamond representing convergence and multi-disciplinary expertise
- **Rules:** No stock photos of people/handshakes/robots. Prefer abstract, geometric imagery that echoes the 4-part diamond motif.

## NOTES FOR GENERATION

### Color Usage
- **Navy (#0A192F):** Primary color - use for most icons and backgrounds
- **Gold (#C9A962):** Accent color - use sparingly for premium feel, logo accents
- **Signal Blue (#3B82F6):** Digital accent - use for CTAs, links, interactive elements
- **White:** Use for text on dark, and as one logo segment color

### Brand Consistency Tips
- Always specify exact hex colors in prompts
- Request "transparent background" or "PNG" for icons
- Reference the "4-part diamond" or "pinwheel" motif when appropriate
- Use "no gradients, flat design" to maintain brand consistency
- Add "institutional, professional, corporate" to maintain tone
- Avoid prompts mentioning "futuristic" or "sci-fi" - keep grounded
- Test generated images in both light (#F1F5F9) and dark (#0A192F) contexts
- The diamond/4-triangle motif should appear subtly throughout, not just in the logo

### Logo Recreation Tips
When generating the logo, emphasize:
- Diamond shape rotated 45 degrees (point at top)
- 4 triangular segments meeting at center point
- Pinwheel arrangement (each triangle points clockwise)
- Colors: Navy (2 segments), Gold (1 segment), White (1 segment)
- Clean geometric edges, no rounded corners on the diamond
