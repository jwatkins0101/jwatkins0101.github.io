Supplement A: Go/No-Go Decision Framework

The Recommendation Spectrum

Phase 1 ends with a clear recommendation. Not hedged consultant-speak—a position.

RECOMMENDATION SPECTRUM

GREEN LIGHT

"Proceed"

AI upside is credible and achievable

YELLOW LIGHT

"Proceed if..."

AI upside exists but depends on specific conditions

RED FLAG

"Reconsider"

AI upside is unlikely or risks outweigh

Decision Matrix

Score each dimension 1-5, then use the matrix below:

Dimension Score 1 (Poor) Score 3 (Moderate) Score 5 (Strong)
Opportunity Size <25 bps EBITDA potential 25-75 bps potential >75 bps potential
Data/Systems Readiness Major gaps, 12+ months to fix Fixable in 6 months Ready now or minor fixes
Process Standardization Chaotic, tribal, undocumented Partially documented SOPs exist, consistent
Management Readiness Resistant, low tech literacy Open but inexperienced Eager, some tech savvy
Industry Tailwinds AI disruption threat, behind peers Neutral, at parity AI opportunity, ahead of peers
Investment Feasibility >$200K+ required, long payback $100-200K, 12-18mo payback <$100K, <12mo payback

Total Score Interpretation

Score Recommendation Language
25-30 GREEN LIGHT "AI-driven value creation is credible. Proceed with confidence. Quick wins available."
18-24 YELLOW LIGHT "AI upside exists but depends on [specific conditions]. Proceed if [conditions] are met."
12-17 YELLOW/RED "Limited AI upside in near term. Proceed only if thesis doesn't depend on operational improvement."
6-11 RED FLAG "AI-driven value creation is unlikely within typical hold period. Significant structural barriers."

"Do This Deal If..." / "Don't Do This Deal If..." Rubric

DO THIS DEAL IF (AI Lens):

  • Management is open to change and has some tech-savvy leaders — Execution depends on people, not just tools
  • Core data exists and is reasonably accessible (even if messy) — Can't automate what you can't access
  • At least 2-3 clear quick wins with <6 month payback — Early wins build momentum and fund further investment
  • Industry is early/emerging in AI adoption (target not far behind) — Room to gain advantage, not just catch up
  • Margin thesis doesn't require AI (AI is upside) — AI is a value creation lever, not the entire thesis
  • Investment required is reasonable relative to EBITDA (<5% of EBITDA for Year 1) — ROI math works

DON'T DO THIS DEAL IF (AI Lens):

  • Management is actively resistant to technology change — You can't force transformation on an unwilling team
  • Data is locked in inaccessible systems or doesn't exist — No data = no AI; this is a multi-year fix
  • Industry is being disrupted by AI-native competitors — Target may be structurally disadvantaged
  • Margin thesis DEPENDS on AI savings that aren't credible — Deal model is broken
  • No quick wins—everything requires 12+ month foundation work — No early momentum; execution risk too high
  • Required investment exceeds realistic appetite — Math doesn't work

Red Flag Triggers (Any One = Serious Concern)

Red Flag What It Looks Like Why It Matters
Data Black Hole Core business data is in inaccessible legacy systems with no APIs, or simply doesn't exist in digital form AI requires data; without it, you're building foundation for 12-18 months before any AI value
Active Resistance Management explicitly dismisses technology; "we've tried that"; hostile to outside ideas Can't force transformation; will burn money and goodwill
Industry Disruption AI-native competitors taking share; customers demanding capabilities target can't provide Structural disadvantage that AI catch-up won't solve
Regulatory Blocker Industry has emerging AI regulations target can't meet; compliance gaps Liability risk; may block AI use cases entirely
Key Person Dependency All process knowledge in 1-2 people who are resistant or leaving Can't document/automate what people won't share

Yellow Flag Triggers (Proceed with Conditions)

Yellow Flag Condition for Proceeding
Data quality is poor but data exists Budget for 3-6 month cleanup before AI work
Management is skeptical but not hostile Plan for change management; budget extra time
No quick wins, but foundation buildable Adjust timeline expectations; Year 2+ value creation
Industry is advanced; target is behind Invest aggressively or accept competitive risk
Investment required is high Confirm fund appetite before modeling value

Recommendation Template Language

GREEN LIGHT

"Based on our assessment, AI-driven value creation is credible for [Target]. We identified [X] bps of EBITDA improvement potential ($[Y] annually) through [list top opportunities]. The target has adequate data foundations, willing management, and several quick wins achievable within the first 100 days. We recommend proceeding with the AI value creation thesis as modeled."

YELLOW LIGHT

"AI-driven value creation is possible for [Target], but depends on [specific conditions]. We identified [X] bps potential, but achieving it requires [condition 1] and [condition 2]. We recommend proceeding if [conditions] can be confirmed/addressed. If not, the AI value thesis should be reduced to [lower estimate] or removed from the model."

RED FLAG

"We do not believe AI-driven value creation is credible for [Target] within a typical hold period. [Specific barriers]. The deal may still be attractive on other merits, but operating margin improvement through AI should not be modeled. If the investment thesis depends on operational efficiency gains, we recommend additional diligence on non-AI levers or reconsidering the opportunity."

Supplement B: Industry AI Headwinds/Tailwinds Framework

Why This Matters

Industry context determines whether AI is:

  • A tailwind (opportunity to gain advantage)
  • A headwind (threat from competitors/disruptors)
  • Neutral (neither significant advantage nor threat)

This affects both the urgency and the risk of the AI investment.

Headwinds/Tailwinds Assessment Framework

Evaluate each dimension. Net score determines overall industry context.

Dimension Tailwind (+) Neutral (0) Headwind (-)
Customer Expectations Customers increasingly expect AI-enabled experiences; differentiation opportunity No significant AI expectations Customers demanding AI capabilities target can't provide
Competitor Adoption Competitors slow; first-mover advantage available Competitors at similar stage Competitors ahead; catching up required
New Entrants AI barriers protect incumbents No AI-related entry dynamics AI-native startups disrupting market
Cost Structure AI can dramatically improve target's cost position vs. industry AI impact on costs similar to peers Competitors achieving AI cost advantages
Business Model AI enables new revenue streams or models No business model disruption AI threatening core business model
Labor Market AI reduces dependence on scarce labor Neutral labor dynamics Competitors using AI to poach talent advantage
Regulatory Environment Regulations favor AI adoption or are neutral No AI-specific regulation Emerging AI regulations create compliance burden

Scoring & Interpretation

Tally: Tailwinds minus Headwinds

Net Score Interpretation Implication
+4 or more Strong Tailwind AI investment is offensive—opportunity to gain significant advantage
+1 to +3 Moderate Tailwind AI investment is opportunistic—good ROI available
-1 to +1 Neutral AI investment is operational—efficiency play, not strategic
-2 to -3 Moderate Headwind AI investment is defensive—necessary to maintain position
-4 or worse Strong Headwind AI investment is urgent—survival may depend on rapid catch-up

Decision Trees for Common Situations

Decision Tree 1: Can't Get Data Samples

Data samples requested but not received by Day 3? │ ├─► Contact received but delayed │ └─► Extend deadline to Day 4; proceed with interview-based assessment │ └─► Note in memo: "Assessment based on management interviews; │ data quality not independently verified" │ ├─► Contact unresponsive │ └─► Escalate to Engagement Lead → Deal team escalation │ └─► If still no data by Day 4: │ └─► Flag as YELLOW FLAG in memo: "Unable to verify data quality" │ └─► Target refuses to provide └─► Escalate immediately to Engagement Lead └─► RED FLAG: "Target unwilling to share data samples; unable to assess automation readiness"

Decision Tree 2: Interview Reveals Major Red Flag

Interview reveals potential RED FLAG? │ ├─► Document specific quotes/evidence │ ├─► Raise in next daily standup (don't wait) │ ├─► Team decides: │ │ │ ├─► Needs more investigation │ │ └─► Add follow-up questions for remaining interviews │ │ │ ├─► Confirmed RED FLAG │ │ └─► Alert Engagement Lead → Engagement Lead calls deal team │ │ └─► Deal team decides: continue, pause, or adjust scope │ │ │ └─► False alarm / explainable │ └─► Document explanation; continue

Decision Tree 3: Industry Research Inconclusive

Can't determine industry AI maturity level? │ ├─► Searched all standard sources? │ │ │ ├─► No → Complete research checklist first │ │ │ └─► Yes → Escalate to PE/Finance Lead │ │ │ ├─► PE/Finance Lead has industry knowledge │ │ └─► Use their assessment + document source as "team expertise" │ │ │ └─► No one has industry knowledge │ └─► Call deal team: "Do you have industry research or │ contacts who could help us assess AI adoption in this space?" │ │ │ └─► If no help available: │ └─► Document as: "Industry AI maturity unclear; │ limited public information available. │ Recommend further industry diligence if │ AI thesis is material to deal."

Decision Tree 4: Opportunity Economics Don't Add Up

Calculated savings seem too high or too low? │ ├─► Too high (>100 bps from single opportunity) │ │ │ └─► Sanity check: │ ├─► Is the baseline cost correct? │ ├─► Is the realization factor applied? (50-70%) │ ├─► Does this pass the "would I bet my fee on this?" test? │ │ │ └─► If still high → Discuss with PE/Finance Lead │ └─► Either: Find the error, OR document as "upside case only" │ └─► Too low (<10 bps total across all opportunities) │ └─► Sanity check: ├─► Did we identify all opportunities? ├─► Are we being too conservative? ├─► Is this company already efficient? │ └─► If truly low → This is a valid finding └─► Memo should note: "Limited AI value creation potential identified. This may indicate operational maturity or structural constraints on automation."
