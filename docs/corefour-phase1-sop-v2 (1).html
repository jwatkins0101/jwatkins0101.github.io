The Core Questions We Answer

Phase 1 answers four questions for the PE fund:

# Question Why It Matters
1 Can we credibly model operating margin improvement from AI? Feeds directly into the deal model and value creation thesis
2 What are the AI quick wins? Identifies low-hanging fruit for early post-close wins
3 What investment is needed to achieve them? Validates that returns justify the effort
4 How does the target compare to industry AI maturity? Assesses competitive risk and opportunity
Note: Many targets won't be making any AI claims. That's fine—most LMM companies aren't. Our job is to assess what's possible, not validate what's claimed.

Reframing the Engagement

Old Framing (Claim Validation) New Framing (Opportunity Sizing)
"Management says X about AI—is it true?" "Given this company's processes, data, and systems, what AI-driven margin improvement is credible, and what would it take to get there?"

Role Primary Responsibilities

Pre-Engagement Checklist

Materials to Request from PE Fund

  • CIM (Confidential Information Memorandum)
  • Historical financials (3 years P&L, balance sheet)
  • Quality of Earnings report (if available)
  • Deal model (so we understand their margin assumptions)
  • Investment thesis memo (what's the value creation plan?)

Materials to Request from Target Company

  • Organization chart with headcount by function
  • Systems/software inventory list
  • Sample reports they run regularly (financial, operational)
  • Process documentation (if any exists—often doesn't)

Key Context to Get from Deal Team Before Starting

  • What margin improvement are they modeling? (e.g., "200 bps EBITDA expansion")
  • What's the hold period assumption?
  • What's their appetite for technology investment?
  • Any specific areas they want us to focus on?

DAY 1: Immersion & Initial Interviews

Morning (4 hours)

All Team — CIM Deep Read (2 hours)

Read with these lenses:

  • Where is labor concentrated? (headcount by function)
  • What processes seem complex or manual?
  • Where is gross margin leakage likely?
  • What's the cost structure? (fixed vs. variable, SG&A breakdown)

Team Huddle (30 min)

Create initial hypothesis list:

  • "Finance team is 6 people for $40M revenue—likely manual processes"
  • "Sales is relationship-driven, no mention of CRM or pipeline tools"
  • "Ops mentions 'custom' work—probably low standardization"

Engagement Lead + PE/Finance Lead — Fund Alignment Call (30 min)

Critical questions for deal team:

  • What margin improvement is in your model?
  • Where do you think the operating leverage comes from?
  • What would make you more/less confident in the value creation thesis?
  • Any concerns about the target's ability to change?

Technical Lead + Analyst — Systems & Org Analysis (1 hour)

From CIM and any materials provided:

  • Map headcount by function
  • List known systems
  • Identify gaps (e.g., "no CRM mentioned")

Afternoon (4 hours)

All Team — Management Interview #1: CEO/President (90 min)

REVISED INTERVIEW PROTOCOL — OPPORTUNITY-FOCUSED
Opening (10 min)
  • Introductions, explain our role
  • "We're here to understand your operations and identify where AI/automation could create value post-close. We're not auditing or judging—we're looking for opportunities."
Business Understanding (20 min)
  • Walk us through your core business process from customer inquiry to cash collection
  • What are the most time-consuming activities in your business?
  • Where do you feel like you're leaving money on the table?
  • What would you do with 20% more capacity without adding headcount?
Process Deep Dive (25 min)
  • How do you generate and close new business? (sales process)
  • How do you fulfill orders/deliver services?
  • How does your finance team operate? How long is month-end close?
  • What's your biggest operational headache?
Data & Systems (15 min)
  • What systems does your team live in day-to-day?
  • Where does your most important data live?
  • What can't you easily see or report on?
Change Readiness (15 min)
  • If a new owner wanted to implement new tools or processes, how would your team respond?
  • Have you tried implementing new technology before? What happened?
  • Who are your most tech-savvy people?
Closing (5 min)
  • What should we make sure to understand about your business?
  • Who else should we talk to?

Post-Interview Debrief (30 min)

Each person notes:

  • Processes that sounded manual/inefficient
  • Functions with high headcount relative to output
  • Resistance signals or openness to change
  • Quick win candidates

Day 1 Deliverables

  • CIM analyzed, hypotheses documented
  • Deal team alignment complete
  • CEO interview complete
  • Initial opportunity hypotheses listed

DAY 2: Deep Interviews & Functional Analysis

Morning (4 hours)

All Team — Management Interview #2: CFO/Controller (60 min)

Focus: Finance function efficiency and data quality

  • Walk me through month-end close step by step. How long does each part take?
  • How many people touch the close process?
  • How do you build reports for the board/ownership?
  • What reconciliations are most painful?
  • Where do you spend time that feels like it shouldn't require your expertise?
  • What data is hardest to get or trust?

All Team — Management Interview #3: Head of Sales or Operations (60 min)

Focus: Revenue process and operational efficiency

For Sales:

  • How do leads come in? How do you track them?
  • Walk me through creating a quote or proposal
  • How do you know what's in your pipeline?
  • What's your follow-up process with prospects?

For Operations:

  • How do you schedule work/production?
  • How do you track job status or project progress?
  • Where do things get stuck or delayed?
  • What's manual that you wish was automated?

Technical Lead + Analyst — Systems Deep Dive (2 hours)

Create comprehensive systems inventory:

System Function Users Data Quality Integration Automation Potential
QuickBooks Accounting 3 Medium Bank feed only Medium — close automation
Excel Everything else 20 Low None High — replace with proper tools
Email Sales, support All N/A None High — CRM + sequences

Afternoon (4 hours)

PE/Finance Lead — Industry AI Maturity Analysis (3 hours)

This is critical and different from generic "competitor scan." We're assessing:

Dimension Questions to Answer Sources
Adoption Breadth How widely is AI used across business functions in this industry? Industry reports, case studies, vendor marketing
Adoption Depth Is it surface-level (chatbots) or operational (core processes)? Job postings, tech stack analyses
Leaders vs. Laggards Who are the AI leaders in this space? What are they doing? Press releases, LinkedIn, conferences
Table Stakes vs. Differentiator Is AI becoming required to compete, or still a differentiator? Customer expectations, RFP requirements
Typical Timeline How long does it take peers to implement meaningful AI? Case studies, vendor claims
Output: Industry AI Maturity Scorecard
Factor Assessment Evidence
Industry AI adoption Early / Emerging / Mainstream / Mature [Cite sources]
AI in core operations Rare / Occasional / Common / Standard [Examples]
AI in sales/marketing Rare / Occasional / Common / Standard [Examples]
AI in finance/admin Rare / Occasional / Common / Standard [Examples]
Target's position vs. industry Behind / At Parity / Ahead [Comparison]
Catch-up timeline estimate [X months to reach parity] [Rationale]

Technical Lead — Data Quality Assessment (1 hour)

When data samples arrive, assess not just quality but automation readiness:

System Data Quality Joinability Automation Readiness Notes
CRM Low (50% complete) Poor Low — needs cleanup first 6-8 weeks of data hygiene needed
ERP Medium Good Medium Can automate with some prep
Accounting High Good High Ready for automation

Day 2 Deliverables

  • CFO and Sales/Ops interviews complete
  • Systems inventory with automation readiness
  • Industry AI maturity scorecard complete
  • Data quality and readiness assessment

DAY 3: Opportunity Identification & Sizing

This is the critical day. We're building the margin bridge.

Morning (4 hours)

All Team — Opportunity Identification Session (2 hours)

Bring together all interview notes and analysis. For each function, identify:

Function Current State Opportunity Type Confidence
Finance 5-day close, 2 FTEs Reduce to 2-day close Efficiency High
Sales Manual quotes, 3 hrs each Automate quoting Efficiency + Speed High
Ops Excel scheduling Scheduling tool Efficiency Medium
Sales No outbound process Add automated outbound Revenue Medium

Categorize opportunities:

  1. Efficiency Gains — Same output, less cost/time
  2. Capacity Expansion — More output, same cost
  3. Revenue Enhancement — Better conversion, faster cycles
  4. Risk Reduction — Fewer errors, better compliance

PE/Finance Lead — Opportunity Economics Modeling (2 hours)

For each opportunity, model the economics:

Opportunity Current Cost Improved Cost Annual Savings Confidence Timeline
Close automation $180K (2 FTEs) $90K (1 FTE + tools) $90K High 6 months
Quote automation $75K (time value) $15K $60K Medium 4 months
IMPORTANT: Be conservative. Use 50-70% realization factors. If you think savings is $100K, model $50-70K.

Afternoon (4 hours)

All Team — Margin Bridge Construction (2 hours)

Build the "AI Value Creation Bridge" — this is the core deliverable for the deal team.

CURRENT STATE
├── Revenue: $40M
├── EBITDA: $4M (10% margin)
└── Key cost drivers: [list]

AI-ENABLED IMPROVEMENTS (Conservative Case)

1. Finance Efficiency
   ├── Opportunity: Close automation, reporting automation
   ├── Investment: $50K implementation + $20K/year tools
   ├── Savings: $75-100K/year (0.6-0.8 FTE equivalent)
   ├── Timeline: 6 months to full run-rate
   └── Confidence: High

2. Sales Productivity
   ├── Opportunity: Quote automation, CRM implementation, outbound sequences
   ├── Investment: $40K implementation + $15K/year tools
   ├── Savings: $50-75K/year (time value) + potential revenue lift
   ├── Timeline: 4-6 months
   └── Confidence: Medium

3. Operations Efficiency
   ├── Opportunity: Scheduling, status visibility
   ├── Investment: $30K implementation + $10K/year tools
   ├── Savings: $40-60K/year
   ├── Timeline: 6-9 months
   └── Confidence: Medium

TOTAL CONSERVATIVE CASE
├── Annual run-rate savings: $165-235K
├── EBITDA impact: +40-60 bps
├── Total investment (Year 1): $120-150K
├── Payback: 6-10 months
└── Confidence-weighted estimate: $180K (+45 bps)

TOTAL BASE CASE (Higher confidence in execution)
├── Annual run-rate savings: $250-350K
├── EBITDA impact: +60-90 bps
└── Requires: Strong execution, management buy-in

Technical Lead — Investment Requirements (1 hour)

Category Description One-Time Cost Annual Cost Timeline
Tools/Software CRM, automation platform, BI $15-25K $30-50K Immediate
Implementation Configuration, integration, training $60-100K — 3-6 months
Data Cleanup CRM hygiene, master data $20-40K — 2-3 months
Change Management Training, adoption support $15-25K — Ongoing
TOTAL $110-190K $30-50K

Analyst — Quick Wins List (1 hour)

Quick Wins Criteria:

  • Can start within 30 days of close
  • Visible results within 90 days
  • Low complexity / low risk
  • Builds momentum for bigger initiatives
Quick Win Function Effort Impact Timeline Why First
CRM implementation Sales Medium High 60-90 days Foundation for everything else
AP automation Finance Low Medium 30-60 days Immediate time savings, visible
Reporting dashboard Finance Low Medium 45-60 days CFO loves it, visible to board

Day 3 Deliverables

  • Opportunity list with economics
  • Margin bridge (conservative + base case)
  • Investment requirements detailed
  • Quick wins prioritized

Determine:

  • Where does the target sit?
  • What's the risk of staying there?

Analyst — Full Memo Draft (2 hours)

Draft all sections using the memo template.

Engagement Lead — Executive Summary (1 hour)

Write the "so what" section:

  • Headline finding
  • Credible margin improvement range
  • Key dependencies/risks
  • Investment required
  • Recommendation

Afternoon (4 hours)

All Team — Internal Red Team (2 hours)

Challenge every number:

  • Is the margin bridge defensible?
  • Are we being too optimistic or pessimistic?
  • Would the deal team's operating partners believe this?
  • What would make us wrong?

Stress Test Questions:

  • "What if they only capture 50% of the savings?"
  • "What if implementation takes twice as long?"
  • "What if the team resists change?"

Analyst — Memo Revision (2 hours)

Incorporate feedback, finalize draft.

Interview Questions

For Targets with NO AI Claims (Most Common)

Framing: "We're looking for opportunities, not auditing what you've done."

Process Efficiency Questions

  • What takes longer than it should in your business?
  • Where do your best people spend time on low-value work?
  • What would you automate if you could wave a magic wand?
  • Where do things fall through the cracks?

Capacity Questions

  • If your team had 20% more capacity tomorrow, what would you do with it?
  • What can't you do today because you don't have the bandwidth?
  • Where are you turning away business or saying no to customers?

Data & Visibility Questions

  • What decisions do you wish you had better data for?
  • What reports do you need that you can't easily get?
  • Where do you have to "go find" information instead of it coming to you?

Change Readiness Questions

  • If a new owner wanted to implement new tools, how would your team respond?
  • Who are your most adaptable people?
  • What's been tried before that didn't stick?

For Targets with AI Claims (Less Common)

Add these if they mention AI/automation:

  • Tell me about [the AI initiative]. What does it actually do?
  • What results have you seen?
  • Who built it / maintains it?
  • What didn't work as expected?

Rules of Thumb

Conservative Estimation Guidelines

Opportunity Type Typical Range Realization Factor
FTE efficiency (reduce headcount) $50-80K per FTE 50-60%
FTE efficiency (same headcount, more output) 20-30% of FTE cost 60-70%
Time savings (not FTE reduction) $50-75/hour value 40-50%
Error reduction Varies by impact 50-60%
Revenue acceleration Highly variable 30-50%

Investment Estimation Guidelines

Component LMM Range
CRM implementation (HubSpot/Salesforce) $25-50K
Accounting automation $15-30K
Custom AI automation (per use case) $30-75K
Data cleanup / migration $20-50K
Training / change management $15-25K
Annual software costs $20-60K

Remember: This SOP is designed for targets that aren't making AI claims—which is most of them. The value is in showing the deal team what's possible, not debunking what's claimed.
